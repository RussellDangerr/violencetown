# Factory

> *Machines, conveyor belts, smokestacks. The Robot was built here. Goo pipelines run through everything.*

Industrial zone. The Factory's toxic machines produce Goo as a byproduct. Aliens arrived to steal it — they eat it.

## Element: Goo

Green, radioactive, alien. Contact fills the bar. At high levels, **you become 2× fast** — but the game is harder because you're moving too fast to think. Powerful and dangerous to yourself.

Goo and Sludge oppose each other on the same seesaw spectrum. Stepping in Goo pushes you away from Sludge. The middle is safe.

## Grey Alien Rescue (Abe's Oddysee)

Grey aliens are chained throughout the Factory, forced to haul Goo. Free them and they follow you, fight for you. Simple follow/wait command system. Lead to extraction points. The more you free, the weaker the green commanders become. Rescuing greys IS the path to beating the boss.

Everything about the greys is grey — skin, mood, outlook, living conditions.

## Environmental Hazards

- **Goo pools** — Step in them, bar goes up. Powers you up AND costs you.
- **Goo vats** — Multi-tile containers. Proximity fills bar passively.
- **Goo pipes** — Breakable. Smash to create new pool tiles. Tactical or accidental.
- **Conveyor belt tiles** — Push you in a direction. Can push you into Goo.
- **Crusher tiles** — Timer-based. Instant damage.
- **Electric gate tiles** — Toggle on/off. Blocks + zaps.
- **Feeding troughs** — Where green men eat Goo. High radiation.

**Safe tiles:** Maintenance tunnels, control rooms (shut off machines from here), grey alien holding cells.

## Sub-Areas

- **Production floor** — Conveyor belts, machinery.
- **Goo vats** — Processing area. Highest Goo concentration.
- **Alien-occupied executive level** — Where the green commanders rule.
- **The spaceship** — Docked into the Factory. Classic UFO design.
