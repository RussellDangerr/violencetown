# Sewer

> *Dark, wet, claustrophobic. Everything is lit by the purple glow of sludge rivers.*

Underground drainage zone beneath [[Zones/Street|Street]]. Home to wererats, fungi, and [[Creature Cards/Loch Ness Monster|Lonny]].

## Element: Sludge

Purple, glowing, void-like. Contact-based. Steps on sludge tiles fill the Sludge bar. At high levels, enemies become **2× fast** — the world speeds up around you.

**Cure:** Soap + water at shower basins. Soap is the Sewer's most valuable commodity.

## Map Style

Pipe Network — maze of interconnected tunnels. Tight corridors, branching paths, dead ends. Rat-sized shortcuts (1-tile gaps) that only Rat form can use.

## Environmental Hazards

- **Sludge pools** — Static purple tiles. Step on them, bar goes up.
- **Sludge flows** — Moving tiles that push you downstream.
- **Sludge drips** — Ceiling tiles, periodic. Timed hazard.
- **Sludge geysers** — Periodic eruptions, spray radius.
- **Burst pipes** — Permanent hazard blocking corridors.

**Safe tiles:** Shower basins (use soap here), clean water pipes (breakable), dry ledges.

## Sub-Areas

- **Soap Mine** — Central region. [[Violet Fungus]] miners haul soap and rock to the [[Fungus King]]'s chest.
- **Wererat Lair** — Northern sealed area. Peek-only in current build.
- **Beholdem's Antechamber** — Eastern sealed area. [[Bosses/Texas Beholdem|Texas Beholdem]]'s "saloon."
- **South Corridor** — [[NPCs/Carrion|Carrion]] blocks the path. Sewer river's swollen with sludge.
- **The Pipe Network** — Where [[Creature Cards/Loch Ness Monster|Lonny]]'s body threads through the infrastructure.

## Key NPCs

- [[NPCs/Carrion|Carrion]] — Dehydrated zombie merchant. South corridor.
- [[NPCs/Behest|Behest]] — The Drain Archive branch librarian (different appearance).

## Enemies

- **Violet Fungus** — Miners. WORKING state. Haul soap to chest.
- **Red Fungus** — Aggressive. Legacy chase behavior.
- **Ghost Fungus** — Phasing. Long sight range. Bribery-immune.
- **Fungus King** — Boss-tier NPC. Barks orders. Bribeable at very high threshold (200).
