# Circus

> *Dusty carnival grounds. Faded striped tents. "SEE THE AMAZING..." attractions. The clowns run the show but the real weirdness lives behind the tents.*

A permanent roadside attraction that never left. Americana cryptid habitat disguised as entertainment. The clowns are non-human — tied to cryptids, carnival folk who don't age and don't eat normal food.

## Element: Fun

Fun fills the bar as clowns entertain, pull pranks, and put on spectacles. **Fun steals your Life.** The circus is a blast and it's killing you.

**Fun Berserk** (at max): Total loss of control. Character does random stuff fast — running, attacking, interacting with everything. The clowns watch and feed.

## Environmental Hazards

- **Confetti tiles** — Burst on step. Fun ticks up.
- **Balloon piles** — Pop when walked through. Satisfying. Deadly.
- **Carnival game booths** — Ring toss, whack-a-mole. You win prizes! Fun spikes.
- **Clown performances** — Juggling, pratfalls, pie-throwing. Proximity fills Fun.
- **Clown attacks:** Pie throw (Fun + minor damage), Honk blast (stun), Tickle ambush (Fun spike + movement lock), Balloon animal gift (takes inventory space, too cute to discard).
- **Cotton candy clouds** — Sticky tiles. Slow + Fun increase.
- **Calliope music tiles** — Ambient Fun fill from proximity.

**Safe tiles:** Backstage areas — behind the curtains. No performances. Dark, quiet. Where you see what the circus really is.

## Sub-Areas

- **The Midway** — Main path. Booths and rides on both sides.
- **The Big Top** — Central tent. Deeper performances.
- **Backstage** — Coffins, blood stores, the real infrastructure.
