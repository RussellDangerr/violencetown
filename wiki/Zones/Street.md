# Street

> *A city where everyone's indoors. Empty sun-blasted streets. Crowded, stuffy interiors.*

The hub zone. Renamed from "Town" during the adventure pivot. The player starts here. The other four zones radiate outward.

## The Pinch

Street is a two-sided trap engineered by vampires:
- **Outside:** Sun scorches you. No real sunblock — only useless [[Moonblock]]. Direct Life drain on exposed tiles.
- **Inside:** [[Boredom]] builds. Stuck indoors with nothing to do, money piling up. Forces random actions at high levels.

Every other zone has one element to manage. Street has two. The vampires designed it this way.

## Sub-Areas

- **The Boardwalk** — [[NPCs/Jersey|Jersey]]'s diner. Neon signs, taffy stands, fortune tellers, arcade machines. Night Kid's home turf.
- **Bank Street** — The financial district. Pale bankers in expensive suits behind tinted windows. Gothic-corporate. [[NPCs/The Financier|The Financier]]'s domain.
- **The Library (Main Branch)** — [[NPCs/Behest|Behest]]'s station. Where skills are learned from books.

## Tile Palette

Concrete, asphalt, brick, storefronts, streetlights, dumpsters. **Shade tiles** (awnings, overhangs, alley shadows) as safe outdoor paths. **Exposed tiles** (open streets, parking lots) as sun damage zones. **Indoor tiles** (shops, offices, apartments) as boredom zones.

## Environmental Hazards

Boredom doesn't come from stepping on tiles — it comes from **boring stuff accumulating as you get richer:**
- **Coins** — Appear on the ground as money increases. A trap disguised as loot.
- **Fax machines** — Printing endlessly. Walking near one ticks boredom.
- **Paperwork piles** — Tax forms, memos, TPS reports.
- **ATMs** — Dispense money. More money = more boredom.
