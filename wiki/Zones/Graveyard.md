# Graveyard

> *Tombstones, crypts, dead trees, fog, iron fences. Quiet and spooky. But underneath the quiet, Death is working.*

The dead part of town. The undead deny death — and something has noticed.

## Element: Death

The bar fills while you're in the Graveyard. [[Bosses/The Deity|The Deity]] is *actually* killing you. Not DoT, not a debuff — **Death**. Full bar = dead. No second chance. The only zone where the environment goes straight for your HP.

## Environmental Hazards

- **Bone piles** — Scattered bones. Step on them, Death ticks up.
- **Bone walls** — Catacomb-style. Proximity ticks Death.
- **Open graves** — A hole with your name on it. Standing on one spikes Death.
- **Gravestones with your name** — The Deity trolling you.
- **Coffin tiles** — Each one ticks Death.
- **Skeletal hands** — Grab at you from below. Death tick + movement penalty.
- **Fog tiles** — Can't see through. Death ticks faster.
- **Candle tiles** — When the candle goes out, Death spikes. The candle IS your remaining time.
- **Death whispers** — Near the Deity's presence. "Come on, bro. The water's fine."

**Safe tiles:** Sacred ground (protective symbols, Death pauses), lit areas (multiple candles still burning).

## Creature Forms

- **Skeleton** — Default form. Bony, rattling.
- **Zombie** — Damaged form. When Skeleton takes enough damage, shifts to Zombie. Flesh and rot replace bone. Different stats.

## Sub-Areas

- **Surface graveyard** — Rows of tombstones, winding dirt paths.
- **Ossuary Codex** — Crypt library. Ancient texts. [[NPCs/Behest|Behest]]'s Graveyard branch.

- [[NPCs/Behest|Behest]] — The Ossuary Codex (black shawl, near-whisper).
