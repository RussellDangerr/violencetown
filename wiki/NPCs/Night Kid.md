# Night Kid

The player. A working-class human. Night-shift line cook at [[NPCs/Jersey|Jersey]]'s boardwalk diner. "Night Kid" is a nickname from Jersey; the real name (if any) is not established.

## The Name

Dramatic irony: named for the *negative* cosmological pole (night), but the true arc carries toward the *positive* (sun, gold). Jersey gave the name as patron-naming, unaware (or aware) of the inversion.

## Arc

Pawn → Probe → Protégé → Successor → Apex. Each step earned, not destined.

**By minute 20:** Hated by vampires (Financier rejection), hated by wererats (poisoning frame), sheltered by Jersey (whose loyalty is in question). Without faction, without status, without trust. Free to explore.
