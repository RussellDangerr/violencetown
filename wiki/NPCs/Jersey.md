# Jersey

The player's first boss. Runs the boardwalk diner. Gives [[NPCs/Night Kid|Night Kid]] the inciting-incident package. Appears human early in the game.

## The Reveal

**Mid-game:** Jersey is the Jersey Devil. He is **knowingly complicit** in the package setup. Long-running political stakes in Violencetown that predate Night Kid.

The reveal of his identity AND his complicity should land *in the same beat*: (a) he's the Jersey Devil, (b) he set you up, (c) he had a reason you might agree with in hindsight — but you don't get your innocence back.

## Tonal Register

Mother 3-style boss-character. Mundane authority with hidden depths. Patron, not gnostic. Provides livelihood, location, identity (the nickname). Benefits from the player's dependence.

## Relationship to [[NPCs/Sun Man|Sun Man]]

Jersey is the **patron** (mundane authority, hidden agenda, home-base). Sun Man is the **gnostic** (mythic figure, narrative gate, destiny). They pull Night Kid in different directions. [[The Sunpyre]] integrates both.
