# Sun Man

A separate NPC mentor. NOT the same person as [[NPCs/Night Kid|Night Kid]]. Visible but unapproachable in [[Zones/Street|Street]] early in the game — the player sees him from a distance, lingering at the boardwalk's edge at sunset.

## Origin

Collateral damage from the moonblock scheme. Used moonblock thinking it was sunblock. Got a catastrophic sunburn. Now emits sunlight from his skin. The vampires' cost-cutting accidentally created their own worst enemy.

## Narrative Gate

**First conversation** triggered when the player has unlocked at least one transformation. From that point, Sun Man becomes the true mentor — teaches the cosmology, explains the sun/blood/gold axes, frames the [[NPCs/The Financier|Financier]] fight as larger than revenge.

## Tonal Register

Disco Elysium-style gnostic mentor. Phantom figure at narrative inflection points. Provides meaning, cosmology, future-self. Benefits from the player's growth.

## The Final Arc

Phase 1: Player + Sun Man vs. [[NPCs/The Financier|The Financier]]. Mid-fight, Sun Man is bitten. His transformation is visible and gradual. He coaches you on how to fight the version of him about to emerge. "Aim for the third heart." "When I scream, that's your window." "Don't apologize."

Phase 2: Night Kid vs. Monstrous Sun Man. The real final boss.

Victory: Compression → [[The Sunpyre]].
