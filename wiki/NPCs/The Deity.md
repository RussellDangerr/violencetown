# The Deity

Not a grim reaper. Not a skeleton god. **A beach boy surfer dude.**

## Philosophy

Life is "the beach." Death is "the salty ocean." He wants everyone to paddle out when their time comes and become sea life — fish, octopus, coral. It's beautiful. It's natural. The cycle.

## Why He's Angry

The Skeletons and Zombies are refusing to leave. They died. Their wave came. They were supposed to paddle out. Instead they're still standing on the sand, rotting, rattling. It's not chill. It's actually really uncool, bro.

## Tone

Speaks casually, surfer slang, but what he's saying is terrifying.

> *"Dude, you gotta let go. The ocean is so sick out there. You're gonna love being a fish. Come on, bro. Stop being dead wrong about being dead."*

## Visual

Board shorts and flip flops among tombstones. Surfboard. Golden beach glow. But when angry — waves, salt, seaweed, the crushing depth of the deep sea behind the beach bro persona.
