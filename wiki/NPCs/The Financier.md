# The Financier

Vampire banker. Operates from Bank Street. Architect of the moonblock conspiracy. The political face of the negative cosmological pole.

## Met Early

Met in the opening minutes when Night Kid delivers the (swapped) package. Kicks Night Kid out; hostility starts here. The *recurring* antagonist — not a final-act reveal. By the time the player fights him, they've been thinking about him for the entire game.

## The Moonblock Scheme

Lobbied the FDA to replace sunblock with moonblock. Two birds:
1. **Humans** can't go outside safely — trapped indoors.
2. **Vampires** hoard real sunblock — they walk freely in daylight.
3. **Wererats** are allergic to moonblock (they don't know this until late game).

## The Gold Card

All currency in Violencetown routes through Bank Street. The [[Gold Card]] is a cashless system the vampires built. Every transaction funds the Financier. Killing him isn't just a boss fight — it's economic liberation.

## Power

Economic, not physical. Deals, loans, leverage, hired muscle, compound interest that compounds your blood. He doesn't fight you — he forecloses on you.

## Visual

Immaculate suit. Slicked-back hair. Unnaturally pale. Red eyes behind designer glasses. Fangs visible only when he smiles. A tube of real sunblock on his desk like a status symbol.
