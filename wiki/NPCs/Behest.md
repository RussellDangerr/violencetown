# Behest

The librarian. Controls what Violencians can learn by controlling what they can read. In a city where violence is the default verb, Behest offers the only alternative: understanding.

## The Name

"At your behest" — at your request. Initially reads as a **title** ("the Behest"), not a proper name. Each zone branch appears to have a different librarian. The late-game reveal is that "Behest" is a proper name — it's the same person.

Each branch's librarian looks, sounds, and behaves like a completely different person. No gimmick. No "we're all sisters." The player accepts five different librarians at face value.

## The Thread

Minute environmental details that travel across all five branches:
- Same tortoiseshell hairbrush on every desk (missing one tooth)
- Same mug with the same chip, different liquid each zone
- One specific book always checked out at every branch
- Same shelving habit: returns spine-out on the leftmost shelf first
- Faintest sulfuric smell near the desk

## Mechanical Function

- Interact to access the skill menu.
- Tells you where to find books you need.
- Tracks your reading history.
- Offers book recommendations based on current stats.
- Disposition starts at +30 (friendly). Bribeable with rare books for restricted section access.

## Personality

Procedural, exacting, patient. Doesn't raise her voice. Doesn't judge your choices. Catalogs everything, remembers everything. The KND rule incarnate: bureaucratic procedure for extraordinary content.

> *"You'd like to learn the art of bone-breaking? Section 614.2, third shelf from the left. Please use a bookmark."*
