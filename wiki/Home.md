## Zones
Five zones radiate from the hub. Each has its own element, creatures, boss, and identity.
- [[Zones/Street|Street]] — The hub. Sun damage outside, boredom inside. Vampires in suits.
- [[Zones/Sewer|Sewer]] — Sludge, wererats, fungi, and something much bigger wedged in the pipes.
- [[Zones/Circus|Circus]] — Cryptid carnival. Fun kills. Bigfoot presides.
- [[Zones/Factory|Factory]] — Goo, robots, enslaved grey aliens. Abe's Oddysee energy.
- [[Zones/Graveyard|Graveyard]] — Death drains you. The Deity wants you to paddle out.

## Lore
- **Moonblock** — The central macguffin. Fake sunblock pushed by vampires.
- **The Gold Card** — Cashless economy. Every transaction feeds Bank Street.
- **Ambro & Necta** — Violencian slang for food and drinks.
- **Violencians** — What the citizens call themselves.
