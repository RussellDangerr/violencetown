# Bigfoot

The biggest, most famous cryptid. The alpha of the carnival. Bigfoot doesn't run the circus — Bigfoot IS the reason the circus exists. The clowns set up around him. He's the main attraction, the origin, the center of gravity.

**Visual:** Massive. Towering. Classic shaggy brown fur, huge footprints. But partially "civilized" by the carnival — wears a **tiny fez hat**. Maybe a vest. Still a terrifying force of nature underneath the costume.

**Personality:** Territorial. Tolerates the clowns because they bring audiences. Outsiders who go too deep past the midway, past the sideshow — that's where Bigfoot is waiting. He doesn't perform. He presides.

**Encounter:** Push deeper than you should. The attractions get weirder. The clowns stop being funny and start being nervous. The footprints get bigger. Then you find him. The main event nobody was supposed to see.
