# The Deity — Boss Fight

When angry, the chill facade cracks. The ocean comes with him. Waves, salt, seaweed, the crushing depth of the deep sea behind the beach bro persona.

**The zone IS the fight.** The [[Zones/Graveyard|Graveyard]]'s Death element drains your Life just by existing there. The Deity doesn't need to swing — he's already killing you. The fight is survival against the environment as much as the boss.

**Transformation unlock:** Defeating the Deity unlocks the **Skeleton** form. The player chooses whether to accept.

**Design question:** Does the Deity's presence bring actual ocean tiles into the Graveyard? Salt water flooding crypts, seaweed on tombstones?
