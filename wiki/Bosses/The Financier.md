# The Financier — Boss Fight

The catalyst of the final boss fight, not its conclusion.

**Phase 1:** Player + [[NPCs/Sun Man|Sun Man]] vs. The Financier. Team-up in the bank vault. Standard vampire-banker combat, elevated by the duo dynamic.

**The Bite:** Mid-fight, Sun Man is bitten. His sunlight fights the vampire corruption — but he's losing. Transformation is visible and gradual.

**The Coaching:** During transformation, Sun Man delivers a combat briefing for the monster he's about to become. Combat tutorial as dying mentor monologue.

**Phase 2:** Night Kid vs. Monstrous Sun Man. The Financier is irrelevant. Sun Man — now a monstrous vampire-Sun hybrid — is the real final boss. The player fights him using the playbook he just gave.

**Victory:** Compression. Sun Man's essence doesn't disperse — the player absorbs it. → [[The Sunpyre]].
