# Texas Beholdem

Lightly inspired by Xanathar from D&D, but obsessed with the **American Southwest**. Cowboy culture, desert vibes, tumbleweeds — in a sewer.

**Pet:** A black armadillo. Don't touch it.

**Weapon:** Holds a royal flush of poker cards in his eyestalks — one card per stalk. Throws them as projectile attacks. Five cards, five stalks, five abilities. The cards ARE his eye beams, reskinned as thrown poker cards.

**Visual:** Massive multi-eyed horror wearing a cowboy hat. Bolo tie. Eyes glow purple from the sludge. Each eyestalk grips a poker card. The armadillo rides on him somewhere.

**Location:** Deep Sewer — the farthest, darkest part. His "saloon."
