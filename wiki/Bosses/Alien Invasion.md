# Alien Invasion

A flying saucer docked into the [[Zones/Factory|Factory]]. Classic UFO design fused with industrial architecture.

**Little Green Men** — The commanders. Small, big-headed, smart, cruel. Comically greedy — Glukkons from Oddworld but alien. Gorge on Goo, bully their slaves.

**Alien blaster guns:** A red button on top with yellow-and-black hazard stripes. The little green men press the button with their tiny finger. Absurd. The most dangerous weapon in the zone operated by a little guy pressing a big cartoon button.

**Grey Aliens** — Large, powerful, enslaved. They haul Goo barrels, operate machinery, take beatings. Grey in every sense — skin, mood, outlook, living conditions. Free them and they become allies. The rescue loop IS the path to beating this boss.

**Tone:** Dark comedy. Rupture Farms energy. Propaganda posters. "Employee of the Month" boards with miserable grey alien mugshots.
