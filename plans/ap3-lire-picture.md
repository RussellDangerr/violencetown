# Lire gets a picture

## Goal

Lire, the lion the Lion Whip's "Hire Lire" trick summons, has always drawn as the flat red
fallback box: no Tiny Dungeon cell is a lion, so `tests/sprite-coverage.test.js` carried him on
its known-unsprited list. Give him a real picture.

## The ruling it rests on

Caelan's art pick for "what should Lire look like for now?" was: **Tiny Dungeon's grey
four-legged beast, cell (4,10), recoloured tawny.** The ruling's own cost note says it is a
beast, not quite a lion; it is a stand-in until real lion art exists.

## How

- `tools/gen_recolor.py` already turns the brown rat, (3,10), into the Carnival's rat variants by
  exact colour swaps. It gains a second base: the grey beast, (4,10). Its two greys (body shade
  and light) are swapped for two tawny tones; the outline and the orange tail are left as they
  are, so the silhouette and the ink are Kenney's own.
- The output is a new packed sheet beside the rat one,
  `game/assets-placeholder/kenney/beastVariants_packed.png` (Kenney Tiny Dungeon, CC0, derived
  cell). The rat sheet is regenerated by the same run and stays byte-identical.
- `game/sprites.js` registers the sheet (Tiny style, packed) and gives `Lire` an
  `ENEMY_SPRITES` entry on it.
- `tests/sprite-coverage.test.js` drops Lire from `KNOWN_UNSPRITED`; a new test file checks the
  beast sheet pixel by pixel against the tool's table, the same way `tests/recolor.test.js`
  checks the rats.

## Acceptance

- Summoning Lire through the wheel shows the tawny beast, not the fallback box.
- Lire is off the known-unsprited list, and the coverage test now requires his sprite.
- A 3440x1440 screenshot of Lire beside the player.
- `npm test`, `balance:check`, the naming grep and `autoplay:check` all pass.

## Out of scope

- Real lion art, or any change to Lire's stats, name, summon copy or behaviour.
- Any change to the rat variants.
- The exact tawny tones are this pass's placeholder choice inside the ruling, flagged for
  Caelan's eye.
