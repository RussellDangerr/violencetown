# WA2 — the Ray Gun gets its own picture

## Goal

The Ray Gun still borrows a Tiny Dungeon staff with a blue crystal, which reads as a magic
staff, not a gun. Nothing in any bundled pack is gun-shaped, so it gets one hand-pixeled 16x16
cell of its own on the Tiny extra sheet, and its sprite entry points there.

## What it rests on

- **The ruling on its look** (Caelan's pick): a hand-pixeled brass pistol, seen side-on, with a
  teal glass coil and an energy-yellow tip that matches the energy hit-splat, drawn in the Tiny
  outline and palette; one hand-made 16x16 cell.
- **WA1** (`plans/wa1-weapon-pictures-match.md`): the Tiny extra sheet is built by
  `tools/gen_tiny_extra.py`, and `tests/weapon-art.test.js` keeps every weapon on a cell of its
  own. This slice extends both the same way.

## How

- `tools/gen_tiny_extra.py` draws the pistol from a small character grid, one letter per
  colour: the dark Tiny outline, three brass shades, three teals for the coil, and the energy
  splat's yellow (`#f5d02a`) for the tip. Every colour but that yellow is one the Tiny packs
  already use. It becomes cell 9, so the sheet grows from nine cells to ten (144 px to 160 px).
- The PNG is regenerated by the tool, never edited by hand.
- `game/sprites.js`: `ray_gun` points at `tinyExtra` cell 9.
- The Lion Whip keeps cell 5 (it was made by recolouring the shape the Ray Gun borrowed); only
  the Ray Gun moves.

## Acceptance

- The Ray Gun draws with its new picture, on the ground and in the bag.
- A 3440x1440 screenshot of it in the bag beside the other weapons, plus a 4x close-up of the cell.
- `npm test`, `balance:check`, the naming grep and `autoplay:check` all pass.

Tests:
- `tests/weapon-art.test.js`: the Ray Gun draws from tinyExtra cell 9; the cell keeps the Tiny
  outline, carries brass, teal and the energy splat's yellow, and is unlike every other weapon
  cell; no two weapons share a cell (already there).
- `tests/visual-breakage.test.js`: the sheet-width pin moves from 144 to 160 px, in its own
  commit, because the behaviour changed.

## Out of scope

- Any other weapon's picture, the generated tier weapons, and the Ray Gun's name, stats or
  description.
- Animation, a muzzle flash, or a held-in-hand pose.
