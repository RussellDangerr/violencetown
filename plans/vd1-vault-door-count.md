# VD1: the vault door says how many it wants

Goal: walking into the locked bank vault door logs its refusal plus a count of the three required
items you carry, in the quest HUD's " (n/total)" style, e.g. "[TEXT] (1/3)".

Acceptance:
- The refusal shows the count (0/3, 1/3, 2/3).
- With all three the door opens as before; no exit markers added (FIN1b kept).
- A test walks into the real door with real keys holding none, then one.

Rulings: VDR1 pick (a) the door wants three things and shows how many you carry. FIN1b: no exit
markers on the locked door.

Out of scope: the refusal text and the exit label stay [TEXT] (his words); single-item gates are
unchanged; the grapple anchor message is untouched.
