# WA1: weapons wear pictures that match them

## Goal

Since the 2026-10-08 icon pass every weapon has a picture, but four of them borrow a cell that
reads as a different weapon: the Gator Tail draws as a spear, the Lion Whip as a staff, the
Fearmur as a hammer and the Pipe as a broad sword. This slice gives those four a picture that
matches what they are, on the ground and in the bag.

## What was picked

Caelan's pick on "which pictures should the weapons wear?": recolour better-matching Tiny cells.

| Weapon | Shape (Tiny Dungeon cell) | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Lion Whip | the wavy shaft (10,10) | leather |
| Gator Tail | the tapered blade (9,8) | gator green |
| Fearmur | the staff (9,10) | bone |
| Pipe | the staff (9,10) | copper |
| Wooden Sword | unchanged (11,8) | unchanged |

The recolours are four new cells on the Tiny extra sheet (`tinyExtra_packed.png`), made by
`tools/gen_tiny_extra.py` the same way as its other cells: a palette swap of a Kenney Tiny cell
(CC0) that leaves the dark Tiny outline alone. The sheet is regenerated by the tool, never edited
by hand.

## Acceptance

- Each of the four draws with its new cell, on the ground and in the bag (both go through
  `itemSprite`).
- No two weapons share a cell. A new test, `tests/weapon-art.test.js`, checks every holdable
  weapon (everything in `WEAPONS`, and every item whose slot is the weapon slot): each has its own
  picture, none is the plain-bag fallback, and no two point at the same sheet and cell.
- The test that pins the Tiny extra sheet's size moves from five cells to nine, in its own commit.
- A 3440x1440 screenshot of the bag holding every weapon, opened through real input, and a close-up
  of the four new cells.
- `npm test`, `balance:check`, the naming grep and `autoplay:check` all pass.

## Out of scope

- The generated tier weapons (`WEAPON_TIERS`). They are kept out of the world and out of the item
  registry, so nobody can hold one yet; they draw as the plain bag until they get art of their own.
- Any other item's picture, and any new name, copy or number.
