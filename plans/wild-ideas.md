Genuinely loose sparks that stayed here:

- 💡 **"It's all store credit."** Lean into gold-points-as-credit-cards: the buyback window is your
  *return window*; a merchant could literally say "store credit only" for certain refunds; a
  Gold-Card-tier joke about "points never expire (the window does)." Pure flavor, but it's the joke that
  makes the buyback mechanic *feel* Violencian rather than gamey.
- 💡 **Items with a "true, freely-chosen" second use.** The Converter (sell 500 / throw for huge damage)
  is the template: an item that reads as junk-or-quest to most NPCs but has a deliberate, discoverable
  alternate use. A design lens for the whole item roster, not one item — worth a pass when the roster
  grows. (Ties to the "surface all options teaches the player" legibility principle.)
- 🌫️ **Oil as a "proper" fuel.** Already parked in `chapter-two-…` Phase 2's breadcrumb (alcohol burns
  fast/weak, oil slow/dense). If oil becomes a real item/upgrade later, it lives here until it earns a
  design pass.
