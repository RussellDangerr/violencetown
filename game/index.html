HOW TO PLAY

You deliver burgers on foot for Borgir — ever since rat-people tore the cataclysmic converter out of your car (or so the boss says). You dream of a Yellow Card: a licence to drive a real Violencetown taxi. First, get the car running.

Move & interact

  • W A S D or arrows — press toward a new direction to turn, press again (or hold) to walk. Hold two for diagonals.
  • Tap / click the ground to walk there (paths around buildings)
  • Tap / click a target → walk over & do the obvious thing (Take / Talk / Attack); long-press or right-click for all options · F opens that list for who you face

Combat & actions

  • Space opens the action wheel anywhere — it pre-aims at the nearest enemy. Double-tap Space to instantly repeat your last action.
  • The wheel is a menu you walk into: ← → spin the ring · ↑ (or Space) go deeper — firing is just going deeper into an action · ↓ (or Esc) back out, closing at the top
  • While aiming, arrows nudge the reticle. If an action would catch a friendly, the wheel asks first — ↑ confirms, ↓ cancels.
  • On touch: tap ✦ to open the wheel. The wheel reads as a compass: tap above the hub to go deeper (fire), left/right to spin, below or on the hub itself to back out.

Inventory

  • The bar along the bottom is always live: Shift+← → changes category, Shift+↑ ↓ steps through items, Enter uses what's showing
  • 1–9 select a slot · Space opens its use overlay · pick Use / Eat / Drink, or Smash when something hostile is next to you (throwing is on the action wheel; giving is in Trade)
  • On touch: tap a category chip to switch, ▲ ▼ to step items, and the item cell to use it. Everything else lives in the Remoticon's ITEMS tab.

Other

  • T wait one turn · P pause · Esc cancel any menu · ? open this help
  • L open the log history · On touch: tap the log strip
  • E acts on whoever you're FACING — trades with a shopkeeper, talks to anyone else, else examines it. Walking into them does the same thing
  • Tab opens the Remoticon (ITEMS · GEAR · QUESTS · MAP · RINGS) and pockets it again · or tap ▤. Inside: [ ] switch tabs, R jumps to RINGS. From the world, C J M open it straight on GEAR / QUESTS / MAP.
  • Items, gear and rings are handled by tapping them inside the Remoticon — tap a slot, then an action
  • ☰ opens the menu (Wait / Cancel / Options / Help / Restart)

Autosaves locally — CONTINUE on the splash resumes your run. Code at github.com/RussellDangerr/violencetown.
